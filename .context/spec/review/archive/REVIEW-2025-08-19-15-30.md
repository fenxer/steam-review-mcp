### Key Features Introduced
1. **Status-Based Routing**: Different workflows for Draft/Planning, In Progress, Completed
2. **Task Identification System**: [P1-T1] format for precise cross-referencing
3. **Immutability Protection**: Completed work cannot be directly modified
4. **Change Log Activation**: Formal change tracking only during execution
5. **ADDENDUM System**: Post-completion modification through new specifications
6. **Cross-File References**: `.context/spec/filename.md` format for navigation
7. **Mandatory Reviewer Identification**: Required reviewer name, role, and date for audit trail
8. **Prevention Mechanisms**: Pre-modification verification protocol to prevent direct edits
9. **Violation Prevention Rules**: No-exception policy for all spec file modifications
